# className Class Details

A className is defined by their exhaustive use of magic.  
# Level 1: Spellcasting  
You have learned to cast spells.  
***Prepared Spells of Level 1+.*** You prepare the list of level 1+ spells that are available for you to cast with this feature. To start, choose two level 1 Class spells.  
The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Class levels. When you reach Class levels 2, 4, 7, 8, 10, 11, 13, 14, 16, 19, and 20, choose an additional Class spell. The chosen spell must be of a level no higher than one-sixth your Class level (rounded up). For example, if you're a level 7 Class, your list of prepared spells can include five Class spells of level 1 or 2 in any combination.  
***Changing Your Prepared Spells.*** Whenever you gain a Class level, you can replace one spell on your list with another eligible Class spell.  
***Spellcasting Ability.*** Constitution is your spellcasting ability for your Class spells.  
# Level 1: Origin Feat  
You have unearthed pieces of knowledge, allowing you to gain one Origin feat of your choice. You gain this feature again at class levels 2, 5, 7, 9, 12, 15, and 18.  
# Level 1: Spring of Magic  
You can tap into the wellspring of magic. This wellspring is represented by Class Points, which allow you to create a variety of magical effects.  
You have 4 Class Points. Whenever you gain a Class level after 1, you gain 4 more Class Points. You regain all expended Class Points when you finish a Long Rest.  
You can use your Class Points to fuel the options below, along with other features that use those points.  
***Creating Spell Slots.*** At the beginning of your turn (no action required), you can transform unexpended Class Points into one spell slot. The cost of creating a spell slot of a given level is a number of Class Points equal to the slot's level. The minimum Class level you must be to create a slot is equal to one less than twice the level of the slot; you can create a spell slot no higher than half your Class level (rounded up).  
Any spell slot you create with this feature vanishes when you finish a Long Rest.  
# Level 1: Magical Restoration  
When you finish a Short Rest, you can regain expended Class Points, but no more than a number equal to half your maximum (round down). Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.  
# Level 4: Ability Score Improvement  
You gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at class levels 8, 10, 12, 14, and 16.  
# Level 19: Epic Boon  
You gain an Epic Boon feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. The Boon of Spell Recall is recommended.  
# Class Spell List  
The class spell list contains spells of levels 1-4. The following are valid inclusions if they are of those levels:  
- Anything on the Warlock spell list  
- Anything on a Warlock subclass spell list  
- Anything on an expanded spell list provided by a species, background, or feat  
- Any spell with the Ritual tag  
- The following spells: *Divine Smite, Speak with Dead, Arcane Eye, Jump, Silent Image, Alter Self, Mage Armor, See Invisibility, Darkvision*
